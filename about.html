🎓 About Student Tools

Helping Students Study Smarter

Student Tools is a free collection of simple and practical tools designed to help students organize their study life and save time.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make useful student tools easy to access in one place. Instead of using different websites for calculations, planning, time management, and project ideas, students can use Student Tools as a simple study companion.

What We Offer

🧮

Study Calculators

Calculate GPA, percentages, and other useful study-related values quickly.

⏱️

Time Management

Use the Pomodoro Timer and Study Time Calculator to organize your study sessions.

✅

Organization

Keep track of tasks and study plans using a simple To-Do List.

💡

Project Ideas

Explore graduation project ideas based on your college, department, and specialization.

✨

Free & Student Friendly

Student Tools is designed to remain simple, accessible, and easy to use. Our focus is providing useful features without unnecessary complexity.

Built With Students in Mind

We believe that useful educational tools should be easy to understand and accessible to everyone. Student Tools focuses on a clean interface, simple interactions, and practical features that students can use every day.
