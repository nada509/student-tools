Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Student Tools. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Student Tools is designed to provide simple educational and productivity tools. Most of the calculators and tools on this website work directly in your browser.

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the main features of the website.

Information You Enter

Information entered into tools such as the GPA Calculator, Percentage Calculator, Study Time Calculator, and To-Do List is primarily processed within your browser.

Student Tools does not intentionally collect or store this information on a personal server.

Cookies

Student Tools may use cookies or similar technologies provided by third-party services. These technologies may be used to improve website functionality, understand website usage, or display relevant advertisements.

Advertising

We may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdSense, to display advertisements on the website.

Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show advertisements based on a user's visits to this website or other websites.

Third-Party Services

Some services used by Student Tools may be provided by third parties. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with.

Children's Privacy

Student Tools is intended for students and general educational use. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Student Tools, you can contact us through the contact information provided on the website.
